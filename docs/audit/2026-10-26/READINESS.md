## Remaining limits and maintenance

- The river network is the Ping basin, not comprehensive Salween/Kok/Burmese-border flood monitoring. A nearby gauge does not certify city-wide conditions. Measured rain, local river thresholds and official notices retain priority over model paths.
- Wind and smoke trajectories are model context, not confirmation that agricultural smoke has arrived or attribution of burning. Surface wind in valleys and cross-border transport require corroborating observations.
- Water imagery supports human inspection of readability and scene change only. It does not estimate calibrated depth, overtopping, road passability or flood extent. Current camera availability must be read from the final live result; all nine stale haze sources still require restored upstream captures or validated replacements.
- Water image previews are mutable source images with an explicit mismatch caveat, not pinned analyzed-frame evidence. FloodDash code and an earlier public GET used a five-minute browser TTL; the live post-reload image reads reconfirmed `public, max-age=300`. A four-hour image freeze was not established; the JSON cache repair does not claim to pin mutable previews.
- The configured ten-minute GitHub standby schedule is not a real-time failover SLA: the latest listed successful runs were 01:43Z, 23:37Z and 21:49Z. The Mac relay remained active. A successful occasional job does not prove ten-minute execution.
- **Unresolved flight-provider cooldown:** ADS-B returned 429 while OpenSky continued serving flight updates. The relay retries ADS-B on its normal 30-second cadence, ignores Retry-After and lacks a provider cooldown; sustained failure would mean about 2,880 attempts/day. This is not a tight immediate retry loop, but it is unnecessary quota pressure. OpenSky failed attempts also retry at that cadence, and overlapping slow ticks are not guarded. Provider-specific attempt clocks, persisted rate-limit cooldown and fake-clock scheduling regressions are the next bounded maintenance item. Healthy relay KV normally avoids the Worker fallback path.
- Built-in relay cadence, persisted water throttles, source-age exclusions and existing standby checks remain. No new paid model workload or model-training claim was introduced. The audit does not establish the exact duration/cause of the earlier reported outage.

The Mac was on AC power with 76% battery, `/Volumes/Data` mounted and 5.5 TiB free. The expired away heartbeat remains paused after the user returned; no new paid workloads or automations were created.

Operational handover still needs named system ownership, a technical operator and a duty officer; the public manual explicitly marks these assignments as pending. Code checks cannot substitute for a staffed escalation path.
